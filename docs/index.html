Privacy & Support Task Tracker App Rev 2026-09-05

This page covers the internal task-tracking app used by FTC Robotics Team 13115 to assign, accept, and track build-season work. It only handles what's needed to run the team's task board — nothing here is sold, shared with advertisers, or used outside the team.

Data we collect

What the app stores

Name & email
From your Google sign-in — used to identify you on the team roster and on tasks you create or are assigned.
Subteam & role
Mechanical, programming, outreach, or strategy, plus whether you're a captain — set once during onboarding.
Task activity
Tasks you create, accept, decline, or complete, and any notes you leave — for example handoff notes when backing out of a task.
Push notification token
A device identifier used only to deliver task and reminder alerts to your phone.
Sign-in activity
Timestamps of when you sign in, used for the team's login-activity log.
How it's used

Running the task board — nothing else

Everything above exists to assign and track build-season work, notify you about task changes, and let captains manage the roster. None of it is sold, shared with advertisers, or used for anything outside the team.

Where it's stored

A private team database

Data lives in a private Azure SQL database operated for this team — it isn't public or indexed anywhere. It's kept for as long as you're an active team member and removed on request.

Your rights

Access, correction, deletion

You can ask to see, correct, or delete your data, or have your account removed from the team, at any time — just email the contact below.

Contact

Questions, bugs, privacy requests

For anything about the app or your data, reach the team directly.
